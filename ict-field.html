Working in ICT

The ICT field contains many different types of work. Examples include software development, system and network administration, cybersecurity and working with data.

My previous MBO programme mainly gave me experience with systems, devices and infrastructure. During HBO-ICT I am also getting more experience with programming and web development.

I do not yet know which ICT role suits me best. One of my goals during this programme is to explore different areas of ICT so that I can discover which type of work fits my interests and strengths.
