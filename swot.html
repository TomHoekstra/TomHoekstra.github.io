My SWOT Analysis

Strengths

My strengths are that I can work quickly and efficiently while remaining accurate under pressure. I am also open and patient when working in groups and try to take other people into account.

Weaknesses

Some of my weaknesses are planning and procrastination. I also sometimes wait too long before asking for help and prefer to avoid unnecessary confrontation.

Opportunities

During HBO-ICT I have the opportunity to discover different areas of ICT, develop new technical and programming skills, gain experience with group projects and internships, and build on the knowledge from my previous MBO programme.

Threats

Possible threats are taking on too much work when group members contribute too little, trying to solve problems on my own for too long, and allowing procrastination to increase my workload.
