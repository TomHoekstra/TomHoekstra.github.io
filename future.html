Where I see myself in the future

At this moment I do not yet have a clear idea of what I want to do after this programme. I find it difficult to already choose one specific job or role because I am still discovering which direction suits me best.

One of the reasons I chose HBO-ICT is that I want to explore different areas within ICT and learn more about what I find interesting. During the programme I hope to gain a better understanding of my own interests and strengths.

In four years I hope to have a clearer idea of which direction I want to take. I also want to have developed enough knowledge and skills to make a well-informed decision about my future career.
