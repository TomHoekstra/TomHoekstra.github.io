Why I chose HBO-ICT

Before starting HBO-ICT, I completed an MBO programme in IT Expert Systems and Devices. This gave me experience with technical environments, systems and infrastructure.

I find technical subjects interesting to understand, but I am still discovering whether ICT is the right direction for me in the long term. One of the reasons I chose HBO-ICT is the opportunity to explore different areas within ICT.

What I want to learn

During this programme I want to improve my programming skills and develop my technical knowledge. I also want to gain experience with different areas of ICT so that I can discover which subjects and types of work suit me best.

My expectations

I expect HBO-ICT to challenge me and introduce me to areas that I have not worked with before. By working on different projects, I hope to get a clearer idea of what I want to do in the future.
